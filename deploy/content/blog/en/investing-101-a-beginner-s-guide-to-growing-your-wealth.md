## Understanding Risk and Return

Every investment carries a degree of risk[cite: 1]. The fundamental rule of investing is that higher potential returns require taking on higher risk[cite: 1].

* **High Risk / High Reward:** Individual stocks can grow rapidly, but they experience sharp price swings[cite: 1].
* **Medium Risk / Medium Reward:** ETFs and mutual funds spread out risk across broad markets, offering steady growth[cite: 1].
* **Low Risk / Low Reward:** Government bonds preserve cash safely but grow slowly[cite: 1].
